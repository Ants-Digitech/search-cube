How fast can a business expect to see movement?

Paid media and landing-page improvements can surface signal within weeks. SEO and content typically compound over 90 days and beyond. The first audit prioritizes quick wins, tracking gaps, and longer-term search opportunities.

What makes SearchCubes different from a standard marketing agency?

The work is organized around measurable acquisition: demand, message, conversion, and reporting. Channels are not treated as separate tasks; they are connected to one growth plan and reviewed against commercial outcomes.

Do we need a large ad budget to start?

No. The starting point is a practical plan: clean tracking, stronger conversion paths, and a budget model that can produce useful signal. Spend should scale after the data shows where quality leads are coming from.

What should be prepared before booking a growth audit?

Bring your website, current marketing channels, rough monthly spend, lead goals, and any reporting access available. If tracking is incomplete, that becomes part of the diagnosis.
