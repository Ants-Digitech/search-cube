Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

How quickly can I expect results? +

Paid ads can show early signal within 2–4 weeks once tracking is clean. SEO is slower — expect meaningful movement in 3–6 months. On the first call I'll be specific about what's realistic for your situation, not just give you optimistic numbers.

If you're new, why should I trust you with my budget? +

Fair question. I take fewer clients so I can give more. I'm trained on how platforms actually work today — not habits from 5 years ago. And I'm transparent about what I know and don't know. If I can't do something well, I'll say so before we start, not after charging you.

Do I need a big ad budget to get started? +

No. We start with tracking and conversion-path fixes — which cost almost nothing but make every future rupee work harder. When we do start paid ads, I'll recommend a budget based on what can generate meaningful data, not just spend money to look busy.

What do I need to prepare for the first call? +

Just bring your website URL, a rough idea of your monthly marketing budget, and your main business goal — more leads, more sales, better quality clients, whatever it is. I can figure the rest out from there.

Can I work with you if I'm outside India? +

Yes — I work remotely over email, WhatsApp, and video calls. Most of what I do is platform-agnostic, though I know the Indian market especially well if that's your audience.
