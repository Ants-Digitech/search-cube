# SearchCubes Homepage Redesign Strategy

## Brand Positioning

SearchCubes is a growth marketing partner for businesses that need measurable acquisition, not marketing activity. It combines SEO, paid media, social, content, analytics, and conversion strategy into one accountable system for leads, visibility, and ROI.

## Core Homepage Message

SearchCubes helps businesses turn scattered digital marketing into a measurable growth system: stronger search visibility, better-qualified leads, cleaner conversion paths, and transparent reporting that shows what is driving ROI.

## Hero Headline Options

1. Turn search, paid media, and social into a measurable growth system.
2. Marketing that turns attention into qualified leads, cleaner conversions, and clearer ROI.
3. Stop buying traffic. Build a performance engine your sales team can feel.
4. Growth marketing for businesses ready to measure every move.
5. From clicks to customers: build a marketing system that proves its value.

## Suggested CTA Copy

- Book a growth audit
- Get my 90-day growth plan
- Find hidden revenue leaks
- See where marketing is wasting spend
- Request a performance review

## Refined Homepage Wireframe

1. Sticky header: logo, Services, Results, Process, Proof, growth-audit CTA.
2. Hero: bold outcome-led headline, short supporting copy, primary CTA, proof metrics, growth signal visual.
3. Growth leaks: show why traffic, ads, content, and reporting underperform when they are disconnected.
4. Services: SEO, PPC, social media, content, analytics, and growth strategy, each tied to business outcomes.
5. Revenue engine: attract, persuade, convert, and scale across the buyer journey.
6. Why choose us: strategy-first planning, conversion-first execution, transparent reporting, weekly optimization.
7. Results/case studies: metric-led proof cards with challenge, action, and outcome.
8. Process: diagnose, prioritize, execute, optimize.
9. Trust: client logos, testimonials, performance snapshots, reporting transparency.
10. FAQ: answer buyer objections around speed, budget, difference, and audit preparation.
11. Final CTA: consultation offer with a short form focused on the prospect's growth problem.

## Desktop Structure

Use wide, scannable sections with strong proof density: hero metrics, split problem diagnosis, three-column services, four-column revenue engine, split differentiator section, three case-study cards, proof explainer rows, four-step process, logo strip, testimonial row, FAQ accordions, and a two-column final CTA with form.

## Mobile Structure

Prioritize speed to conviction: headline, one-sentence promise, primary CTA, two proof metrics, growth-leak diagnosis, stacked service cards, one flagship case study first, compact process, testimonials, FAQ, then the consultation form.

## Expanded Story Flow

1. Make the promise: marketing becomes a measurable growth system.
2. Name the pain: businesses are not short on activity; they are short on connected signal.
3. Show the system: demand, message, conversion, and scale work together.
4. Prove the method: publish case studies with metrics and before-after clarity.
5. Reduce risk: explain the process, reporting rhythm, and buyer questions.
6. Ask for action: offer a growth audit, not a vague consultation.

## Case Study Template

- Client type and market context
- Growth constraint before SearchCubes
- Strategic move made first
- Channels involved
- Metrics improved
- Timeframe
- Quote or decision-maker takeaway

## Image Direction

Use licensed iStock imagery that feels analytical, consultative, and operational rather than generic agency lifestyle photography.

Recommended image slots:

- Hero: analytics dashboard or senior strategist reviewing performance data on a laptop.
- Growth leaks: strategy-room image showing decision-makers reviewing funnel performance.
- Performance tracking: dashboard close-up with KPI, lead, or campaign reporting visuals.
- Client clarity: consultant and business owner reviewing growth opportunities.

Useful iStock search starting points:

- [Marketing strategy laptop](https://www.istockphoto.com/photos/marketing-strategy-laptop)
- [Business analytics dashboard](https://www.istockphoto.com/photos/business-analytics-dashboard)
- [Marketing dashboard](https://www.istockphoto.com/photos/marketing-dashboard)
- [SEO PPC](https://www.istockphoto.com/photos/seo-ppc)

Selection rules:

- Prefer real dashboards, laptops, meetings, and operator moments over abstract megaphones, puzzle pieces, or handshake images.
- Avoid overly staged smiles, obvious stock poses, and generic office scenes with no marketing or performance context.
- Keep crops tight and directional so images support the copy instead of becoming decoration.
- Replace placeholder remote images in `index.html` with licensed iStock downloads before production use.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with outcomes: leads, visibility, conversions, ROI, lower waste, cleaner reporting.
- Avoid vague lines such as "boost your online presence" or "tailored solutions."
- Make each section answer what matters, why SearchCubes, and what action comes next.
- Replace channel descriptions with commercial impact.
- Make reporting and process transparency part of the trust story.
